## Choose the right tool

| Need | Tool |
| --- | --- |
| A simple link or scroll target | Native link/click settings |
| Hover/press appearance, entrance, loop or scroll effect | Inspector effects |
| Events with decisions, variables and multiple actions | [Action Workflow](/docs/action-workflow) |
| Per-property keyframes, curves and text stagger | [Motion Editor](/docs/motion-editor) |
| Related content panels | Native tabs/accordion composition |

Start from a usable static page. Add one behaviour at a time and test it in the visitor preview. Decorative movement should not gate essential content or authorization.

## Hover, press and entrances

Configure hover/press values on a layer and choose transition timing. Entrance presets can fade, translate, scale, blur or reveal content. Custom settings combine offsets and opacity. Spring and bezier curves control the feel. View-triggered entrances can play once or on re-entry.

Loop effects repeat after an entrance. Scroll effects follow progress while entering, crossing or leaving the viewport. A scroll effect is not a time-based clip. Test reduced-motion preferences: published motion should leave content in its readable resting state.

## Overlays

Use a popover, dropdown or modal when a panel should open above the page. Choose the trigger, initial state and close animation. Test Escape and outside-click dismissal. A visual overlay is not sufficient proof of complete dialog accessibility: verify keyboard focus, reading order and focus return in your host page.

## Carousel and marquee

Edit the composition's native children, then configure autoplay, interval and direction. Carousels support arrows, dots, swipe, looping, pause-on-hover and slide/fade/zoom transitions. Test one slide, several slides and long content at narrow widths. Essential text must remain available without chasing a marquee or waiting for autoplay.

## Tabs and accordion

Headers and panels share target identifiers inside the composition. Preserve those relationships after duplication or restructuring. Verify visible state and keyboard behaviour in the rendered page. Do not combine a simple disclosure and another workflow on the same click without checking for conflicting handlers.

## Review before publishing

Test a mouse, keyboard and touch viewport. Try quick repeated input, refresh, client-side navigation and reduced motion. Save and reload to verify persisted settings; a transient canvas preview is not the saved runtime.
